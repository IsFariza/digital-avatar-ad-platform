# Development of a Digital Avatar Platform for Advertising Video Production and Campaign Personalization

## Project overview

This diploma project investigates how existing AI avatar-video platforms could better support scalable and personalized advertising campaigns. The project will review relevant literature, compare selected platforms against advertising needs, and use the findings to define and prioritize design requirements. A Figma prototype will illustrate a proposed campaign workflow.

The project focuses on advertising-oriented platform requirements and design. It is not an effort to build a general AI video generator from scratch.

## Problem

Existing AI avatar and video platforms provide capabilities such as talking avatars, video generation, and localization. However, advertising campaigns can also require campaign personalization and versioning, product–avatar fit, and clear disclosure and trust considerations. The project examines how well selected platforms support these needs and what improvements should be considered.

## Research question

What design requirements can be identified to improve existing AI avatar video platforms for scalable advertising video production and campaign personalization?

## Aim and expected outcomes

The aim is to identify design requirements for improving avatar-video platforms for advertising use.

Expected outcomes:

- A literature review relevant to digital avatars, advertising, personalization, and trust.
- A comparison of selected avatar and video platforms using defined criteria.
- A prioritized set of design requirements and acceptance criteria.
- A prototype illustrating the proposed advertising workflow.
- A verification and evaluation plan.

## Target users

The proposed design is intended to support advertising and marketing teams that need to create and manage personalized advertising videos.

## Scope

The project focuses on comparing selected existing platforms and identifying their strengths, limitations, and advertising-specific design requirements. The comparison considers needs such as personalization, localization, scalability, campaign versioning, product–avatar fit, disclosure, and viewer trust.

The project does not aim to build a general-purpose AI video generator from scratch. The prototype communicates the proposed design; it should not be understood as a production-ready platform.
