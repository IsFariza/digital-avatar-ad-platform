# Literature Review

## 1. Introduction

Digital avatar platforms combine visual representation, speech generation, facial animation and content personalization.

The purpose of this literature review is to understand the technologies behind digital avatars and their possible use in advertising.

The review includes 20 scientific sources.

## 2. Research Question

How can digital avatar platforms support advertising video production, and which technical, user and ethical factors affect their success?

## 3. Review of Scientific Sources

### Study 1 - Muller et al. (2022)
The research examines efficient neural graphics using multiresolution hash encoding.

It provides technical background for efficient visual representation but does not measure advertising outcomes.

### Study 2 - Belanche et al. (2024)
The study compares human and virtual influencers.

It shows that audience responses depend on influencer type and product context.

### Study 3 - Yu et al. (2024)
The research studies emotional displays by artificial intelligence-generated virtual influencers.

It connects different emotional displays with engagement patterns.

### Study 4 - Tastemirova et al. (2022)
The research investigates microexpressions in digital humans.

Small facial movements can influence perceived emotion, sincerity and trustworthiness.

### Study 5 - Luo et al. (2025)
The study examines the fit between digital human avatars and tourism destinations.

Avatar suitability is connected with trust and engagement in the studied context.

### Study 6 - Farooq and de Vreese (2026)
The research examines authenticity judgments of generated images.

Realism and detection information can affect how users evaluate authenticity.

### Study 7 - Liu et al. (2025)
The research reviews three-dimensional reconstruction methods.

It compares geometric reconstruction, neural radiance fields and Gaussian splatting.

### Study 8 - Chen et al. (2025)
The research reviews human reconstruction using three-dimensional Gaussian splatting.

It explains methods and challenges in realistic human rendering.

### Study 9 - Zhen et al. (2023)
The research reviews talking-head generation methods.

It discusses face animation, speech alignment, datasets and evaluation.

### Study 10 - Bigioi and Corcoran
The review examines multilingual dubbing technologies.

Translation, speech timing and facial movement must work together.

### Study 11 - Ye et al.
The study reviews human motion synthesis.

It examines realistic movement, motion prediction and physical consistency.

### Study 12 - Zhang
The research reviews generative video technologies.

It identifies technical challenges involving control, consistency, cost and ethics.

### Study 13 - Yang et al.
The research reviews negative impacts of artificial intelligence.

Important concerns include misinformation, privacy, bias and trust.

### Study 14 - Khalfallah
The review studies virtual influencers.

It identifies authenticity, engagement and trust as important topics.

### Study 15 - Hai et al.
The study reviews artificial intelligence in instructional video production.

It distinguishes fully generated videos from technology-assisted production.

### Study 16 - Viktoratos and Tsadiras
The research reviews personalized advertising.

It discusses targeting, recommendations, privacy and data limitations.

### Study 17 - Khoei et al.
The review explores deep learning models and applications.

It provides technical background for computer vision, speech and generation.

### Study 18 - Sengar et al.
The research reviews generative artificial intelligence applications.

It describes text, image, audio and video generation.

### Study 19 - Hussain et al.
The research studies virtual influencers in marketing.

It identifies engagement, trust, authenticity and ethical challenges.

### Study 20 - Cui and Liu
The review examines virtual human technologies.

It connects computer graphics, speech, behaviour, interaction and user trust.

## 4. Comparative Analysis

The reviewed studies can be divided into three main areas.

### Technical Research
Technical studies focus on:
- Rendering efficiency
- Facial animation
- Speech synchronization
- Human motion
- Video generation
- Multilingual output

### User Experience and Marketing
Marketing studies examine:
- User engagement
- Trust
- Authenticity
- Avatar suitability
- Personalized messages

### Ethical Considerations
Ethical research identifies:
- Privacy concerns
- Misleading content
- Artificial intelligence disclosure
- Bias
- Responsible use of digital identities

## 5. Research Gaps

The literature identifies several areas requiring further investigation:

1. The relationship between technical quality and user trust.
2. User responses after repeated avatar exposure.
3. Cross-cultural differences in avatar acceptance.
4. Problems with realistic motion and speech.
5. Privacy and transparency in generated content.

## 6. Relevance to Our Project

The proposed platform will connect editable advertising scripts, campaign variants and digital avatar video generation.

Human review will remain an important step.

The project will investigate local user needs instead of assuming that findings from other industries apply directly to advertising.

## 7. Conclusion

Scientific research supports the technical possibility of digital avatar video production.

However, realistic appearance alone does not guarantee useful or trustworthy advertising.

A successful platform should consider usability, language, content quality, human control and responsible generation.
