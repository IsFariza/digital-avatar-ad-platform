## 5. Risk Management

### Risk 1: Video Provider Unavailable

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High

Mitigation:
Check provider access and conditions early.

Use a replaceable provider connection.

A clearly labelled sample response may support early demonstrations.

### Risk 2: Incorrect Advertising Content

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High

Mitigation:
Use structured campaign briefs, editable content and human approval.

Evaluate generated content using a quality rubric.

### Risk 3: Component Integration Problems

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High

Mitigation:
Agree on common data structures and processing states before implementation.

Document service contracts.

### Risk 4: Scope Expansion

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: High

Mitigation:
Prioritise essential functions.

Defer model training, advanced targeting and automatic publication.

### Risk 5: Privacy and Consent Problems

Likelihood: Low
Impact: High

Mitigation:
Use fictional data and permitted avatars.

Keep consent records where needed.

Label generated content clearly.

### Risk 6: Schedule Delays

Likelihood: Medium
Impact: Medium

Mitigation:
Review progress against milestones.

Prioritise essential tasks.

Move additional functions to later stages when necessary.
