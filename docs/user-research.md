# User Survey and Preliminary Interviews

## 1. Research Purpose
The purpose of this research is to identify the needs and problems of potential users of a digital avatar advertising platform.

## 2. Target User Groups
Two main groups were considered:
- Public relations office staff
- Media Technologies students

## 3. Preliminary User Discussions

### Public Relations Office Manager
An informal discussion took place in October 2026. The exact date still needs confirmation.

The manager explained that preparing promotional videos requires time for planning, filming and editing.

Possible useful functions include:
- Video creation from text
- Reusable announcement templates
- Script editing
- Downloading video results

The manager expressed interest in digital avatars, but actual time savings have not been measured.

### Media Technologies Students
An informal discussion took place in October 2026. The participant count and exact date still need confirmation.

Students expressed interest in using digital avatars for creative projects.

Topics for further research include:
- Avatar selection
- Appearance options
- Voice and language selection
- Different output formats

These discussions are preliminary and do not represent final system requirements.

## 4. Survey Analysis
The survey received 118 submissions.

One test submission and one response without consent were excluded.

The final analysis included 116 submissions.

### Feature Importance

| Feature | Ratings | High Ratings | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Multiple versions from one template | 115 | 73 | 63.5% |
| Audience-specific messages | 99 | 44 | 44.4% |
| Correct pronunciation | 99 | 42 | 42.4% |
| Editing parts of a video | 97 | 41 | 42.3% |
| Avatar suitability | 98 | 40 | 40.8% |
| Different languages | 98 | 39 | 39.8% |
| Consistent avatar | 98 | 38 | 38.8% |
| AI content notice | 80 | 5 | 6.3% |

High ratings mean scores of four or five. The denominator varies because unsure and not applicable responses were excluded for each feature.

## 5. Main Findings
Creating several campaign versions from one template received the strongest support.

Audience-specific messages, pronunciation, editing and avatar suitability also require further investigation.

In addition, 22 of 116 respondents selected viewer distrust as their main concern.

## 6. Research Limitations
The survey used available contacts, and the response rate is unknown.

Some submissions contained repeated wording and short time gaps.

These observations do not prove that responses are invalid.

The discussions did not measure actual production times or demonstrate platform adoption.

## 7. Next Steps
The team plans to:
1. Complete three to five documented discussions in total.
2. Study existing production tools and problems.
3. Collect examples of recent production tasks.
4. Identify and prioritise requirements.
5. Review the results with the supervisor.

## Conclusion
The research indicates potential interest in digital avatar tools.

Reusable templates and editable advertising content are promising areas for further study.

Additional evidence is required before confirming the final platform requirements.
